Front desk questions

Why are there no audio files?

On purpose. Every exhibit is described, never played. Your memory does the playing, and honestly it's a better speaker.

What counts as a "vanished" sound?

Something people heard every day that nobody under 15 has heard at all. If your little cousin looks confused, it qualifies.

Can I visit in person?

Hush Hall only exists here. It's a museum without walls, which is fitting, since its exhibits don't exist either.
